International Exposure

Sivaguru — EEE | Internship at Taipei Economic and Cultural Center

Our student Sivaguru Thilai Mahenthiran from the Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) department was recommended by Meenakshi Sundararajan Engineering College for an international internship exposure at the Taipei Economic and Cultural Center (TECC), Chennai.

The experience brought together students from partner universities for meaningful international interaction and learning. Sivaguru was also recognized with a Certificate of Appreciation during the TECC gathering held on 7 August 2026.
