Test the core idea

Use AI, AR/VR, Game Development, Cloud, Full Stack and Robotics capabilities to prove feasibility before investing further.

Prototype

Advanced Manufacturing

Make it production-ready

Use EV, sustainability, manufacturing and design capabilities to pressure-test manufacturability, precision and scale-readiness.

MSIIC Across MSGI

One innovation centre. Three powerful learning ecosystems.

MSIIC extends beyond incubation. It connects the academic strength of MSEC, the future-focused learning culture of The NEST School, and the leadership and business orientation of MSSM.

For MSEC

Engineering ideas into industry-ready ventures.

MSIIC gives MSEC students and faculty a structured route from classroom learning to real-world problem solving, product development and entrepreneurship.

  • Entrepreneurship and design-thinking learning through the Learn pathway.
  • Access to AI, AR/VR, Robotics, IoT, Cloud, Drone, PCB and Advanced Manufacturing facilities.
  • Support for student projects, prototypes, hackathons, research translation and technology ventures.
  • ICE-backed industry reviews, technical mentorship and manufacturing guidance.
  • Opportunities to pitch validated ideas through Demo Days and investor networks.
For The NEST School

Helping young learners become curious creators.

MSIIC can serve as an innovation bridge for The NEST School by introducing age-appropriate exploration, design, technology and problem-solving experiences.

  • Innovation exposure through demonstrations, workshops and guided discovery.
  • Early introduction to design thinking, sustainability and real-world applications.
  • Mentor-led experiences in robotics, coding, drones, AR/VR and emerging technology.
  • Opportunities to showcase student ideas and collaborate with MSGI institutions.
  • A pathway from curiosity and experimentation to future entrepreneurship.
For MSSM

Turning innovation into business possibility.

MSIIC and MSSM can work together to strengthen the commercial, strategic and leadership dimensions of emerging ventures.

  • Business-model development, market validation and go-to-market thinking.
  • Mentorship in marketing, finance, operations, strategy and entrepreneurship.
  • Case studies, founder talks, pitch preparation and startup-focused projects.
  • Cross-disciplinary collaboration between management learners and technical teams.
  • Industry, alumni and investor engagement for scalable ventures.
The MSGI advantage

Ideas grow faster when disciplines work together.

Engineering creates the technology. Management shapes the business. School-level innovation builds curiosity early. MSIIC brings these strengths together through shared facilities, mentors, projects, challenges and a common innovation ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Start where you are.

Who can join the Learn phase?

Currently enrolled MSGI students across years and departments. No prior startup experience is required.

Can I join without a validated idea?

Yes. Learn is designed for students who are still discovering problems, exploring ideas and learning how to validate them.

What is the difference between Build and Launch?

Build turns a validated idea into a working product using MSIIC facilities and ICE expertise. Launch connects the finished product with mentors, investors and funding pathways.

Can MSEC alumni apply?

Yes. Alumni founders building technology-driven ventures with MSEC roots can explore the Launch pathway.

How long does the journey take?

The journey can run alongside the degree. Students may progress from Learn to Build after validation and into Launch when the venture is ready.
