AUTONOMOUS INSTITUTION UGC & Anna University

WHAT DOES AUTONOMY MEAN AT MSEC?

Autonomy gives MSEC the academic flexibility to design and continuously enhance learning experiences that respond to changing technology, industry expectations and student aspirations.

Curriculum Flexibility

Industry-relevant subjects, electives and emerging technology areas.

Experiential Learning

Projects, internships, simulations and hands-on learning.

Innovation & Research

Students learn to identify problems, develop ideas and create solutions.

Career Readiness

Communication, aptitude, programming and industry-oriented learning.
