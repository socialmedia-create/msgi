KRS Memorial Trophy

Celebrating Excellence. Inspiring Champions.

The KRS Memorial Trophy is a celebration of talent, teamwork and the spirit of healthy competition. It brings students together through sports and activities that encourage discipline, leadership, perseverance and camaraderie.

More than a competition, the trophy honours the values of participation, determination and excellence, inspiring every student to give their best and create lasting memories along the way.

Waste to Wealth

Collection Drive — Share What You Can. Make a Difference.

A student-led collection drive encouraging the community to donate stationery and gently used clothes to those who need them. The initiative promotes the values of sharing, sustainability and social responsibility, while giving useful items a meaningful second life.

Collect. Share. Reuse. Make an Impact.

Inauguration of MSEC Clubs 2026

Celebrating Student Talent, Leadership & Creativity

The Inauguration of MSEC Clubs 2026 marks the beginning of another exciting year of student-led activities, communities and opportunities across the campus.

The clubs provide students with a platform to discover their interests, collaborate with peers, develop leadership and communication skills, and participate in activities beyond the classroom.

Connect. Create. Collaborate. Lead.

Freedom 250: Great American Authors

Celebrating Stories, Ideas & Literary Excellence

A special literary session celebrating the works and ideas of some of the greatest American authors, inspiring students to explore literature, perspectives and the power of storytelling.

The session was graced by Mr. V. Balakrishnan as the Chief Guest, who shared valuable insights and encouraged students to develop a deeper appreciation for reading, literature and creative expression.

Future of Autonomy in Space

Exploring the Next Frontier of Human Innovation

16 July 2026 | KRS Auditorium, MSGI Campus, Kodambakkam

Space exploration is entering a new era — powered by AI, robotics and autonomous technologies.

Discover how intelligent systems are transforming space missions, planetary exploration and the possibilities of life beyond Earth.

Explore. Imagine. Innovate.

Join us as we look into the future of human–machine collaboration and autonomous space exploration.

Organised by Meenakshi Sundararajan Group of Institutions.
