Placement training

From campus to career — a multi-year journey.

Placement is not a final-year event. MS-CDC blends aptitude development, communication mastery, technical depth, career simulations and personalised mentoring.

01

Foundation

Aptitude & Communication

  • Logical reasoning and quantitative ability
  • Verbal skills and professional communication
  • Early-semester foundation building
02

Technical

Domain Readiness

  • Discipline-specific technical preparation
  • Company-aligned concepts and expectations
  • Mock technical rounds and assessments
03

Interviews

Mock Interviews & HR Training

  • Simulated interview environments
  • Behavioural and HR preparation
  • Feedback from trainers and alumni recruiters

Company-specific preparation

Customised modules based on recruiter profiles, job roles and past interview trends. Students practise role-based technical problem-solving and real-world test simulations.

Performance tracking

Progress is monitored through analytics, test scores and skill benchmarks. Targeted interventions, retests and focused mentoring support learners who need additional help.
