One ecosystem · multiple pathways

Industry is not a destination.
It is part of the learning journey.

MS-IIC creates structured touchpoints between education and the workplace — from early awareness to advanced application and professional contribution.

01 · Exposure

See the real world.

Students encounter practitioners, sectors, workplaces and technologies beyond the classroom.

  • Industry visits
  • Expert talks & practitioner interactions
  • Sector awareness
  • Technology discovery through ICE
02 · Engagement

Work with people from industry.

Learning becomes interactive through mentoring, workshops, internships and industry-defined challenges.

  • Corporate mentoring
  • Short-term internships
  • Skill certifications
  • Competitions & challenges
03 · Application

Solve real problems.

Students move from understanding concepts to applying them in practical engineering, management and technology contexts.

  • Live projects
  • Longer internships
  • Capstone projects
  • Research & consultancy opportunities
04 · Capability

Build professional readiness.

Technical and workplace capabilities develop alongside academic knowledge.

  • Industry training
  • Faculty development
  • Professional communication
  • Technical assessments
05 · Innovation

Create and test.

ICE gives students a technology-rich environment to experiment, build, test and apply emerging technologies.

  • AI & IoT
  • Robotics & Drones
  • AR / VR
  • VLSI & Advanced Manufacturing
06 · Contribution

Enter industry with context.

Students progress towards leadership, recruitment readiness and the ability to contribute professionally.

  • Recruitment engagements
  • Corporate assessments
  • Student leadership
  • Industry & society contribution
