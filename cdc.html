MSEC • ENGINEERING

Engineering to Employability

For engineering students who want to move from academic knowledge to technical confidence, professional competence and placement readiness.

  • 600+ hours of training* across career readiness and placement preparation.
  • Aptitude, quantitative ability, logical reasoning and verbal ability.
  • Technical readiness, coding/software-test orientation and domain preparation.
  • Resume building, LinkedIn/profile development and interview practice.
  • Group discussions, presentations, communication and workplace behaviour.
  • Alumni mock interviews, industry interactions and placement-readiness activities.
  • UPSC awareness, mentorship and competitive-exam pathways through dedicated support.

MSSM • MANAGEMENT

Management to Leadership

For MBA learners preparing to think strategically, communicate persuasively, solve business problems and lead in a changing workplace.

  • Career discovery and role-based pathway planning.
  • Business communication, presentations and executive presence.
  • Resume, interview and group-discussion readiness.
  • Case analysis, problem-solving, decision-making and business storytelling.
  • Industry exposure through live projects, corporate interactions and expert sessions.
  • Professional networking, competitions, clubs and leadership experiences.
  • Higher education and specialised career pathway conversations.

THE NEST SCHOOL • UGCC

UGCC: University Guidance & Career Counselling

For NEST learners and families, UGCC helps turn curiosity into informed choices across higher education, subject pathways and future careers.

  • Age-appropriate career awareness and self-discovery conversations.
  • Subject, stream and programme exploration for IB and Cambridge learners.
  • University discovery across India and international destinations.
  • Course-fit, eligibility, entry requirements and application timeline awareness.
  • Parent–student counselling and decision-making support.
  • Scholarship, portfolio, personal statement and profile-building awareness.
  • University fairs, expert interactions and global education sessions.
  • Application-to-admission milestone planning with direct verification from institutions.

MSEC | Placement Readiness

*Break-up and exact module count should be aligned with the approved MS-CDC training calendar.

What the training can include

From first-year foundations to final-year readiness.

  • Career orientation and goal-setting sessions.
  • Quantitative aptitude, reasoning and verbal ability.
  • Technical assessments and role-aligned preparation.
  • Resume, email, LinkedIn and professional profile building.
  • Group discussions, mock interviews and presentation practice.
  • Alumni, industry and recruiter-facing interactions.
  • Feedback loops to identify gaps and improve performance.
UGCC | Global Higher Education

Explore the University of Nottingham.

The University of Nottingham is a research-intensive university with an international presence across the UK, China and Malaysia. UGCC can help students explore its programmes and understand the steps involved in making an informed application.

  • Explore undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes.
  • Understand course fit, entry requirements and application routes.
  • Learn about research-led teaching and global academic exposure.
  • Explore student experience, employability and international opportunities.
  • Verify current programme details, fees, scholarships and deadlines directly with the university.

UGCC Guidance Principle

We do not choose a future for a student. We help them choose with clarity.

UGCC brings together learners, parents, counsellors, faculty, experts and universities so that important education decisions are informed, realistic and personal.

  • Discover the learner.
  • Understand the options.
  • Compare pathways.
  • Plan the next steps.
  • Review and refine.
