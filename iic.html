About MSIIC

A campus culture where innovation is a practice.

The Meenakshi Sundararajan Innovation & Incubation Centre is designed to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, skill development and collaboration between academia, industry and entrepreneurial ecosystems.

Innovation mindset

Encouraging students to observe, question, empathise and identify meaningful problems.

Entrepreneurial development

Supporting idea validation, business thinking, prototyping, mentoring and venture readiness.

Industry connection

Helping student projects respond to real-world needs through expert interaction and collaboration.

From the Head's Desk

“Innovation grows when curiosity is given the freedom to experiment, the discipline to learn and the support to build.”

At MSIIC, our aim is to create an environment where students are not only consumers of technology, but also thoughtful creators, problem-solvers and future entrepreneurs.

Institution-wise pathways

One ecosystem.
Three learner journeys.

MSIIC adapts innovation and entrepreneurship experiences to the needs of engineering, management and school learners.

MSEC · ENGINEERING

Build technology that solves problems.

For engineering students, MSIIC connects technical learning with design thinking, product development and startup creation.

  • Design Thinking – Building Innovation Mindset
  • 6-semester EDI curriculum
  • Hackathons and product challenges
  • AI, IoT, robotics, AR/VR and emerging technology projects
  • Prototype development and campus-benefit projects
  • Startup mentoring, pitching and industry connect
Explore MSEC MSIIC ↗
MSSM · MANAGEMENT

Turn business ideas into value.

For management learners, MSIIC can create a platform for entrepreneurial thinking, business model design and market-led innovation.

  • Business opportunity identification
  • Customer discovery and market research
  • Business model and pitch development
  • Marketing, finance and operations thinking
  • Social enterprise and sustainable business ideas
  • Founder talks, case discussions and venture exposure

THE NEST SCHOOL · SCHOOL INNOVATION

Grow curious, creative changemakers.

For The NEST School, innovation can begin with curiosity, inquiry, collaboration and age-appropriate problem-solving.

  • Innovation and design-thinking challenges
  • Creative problem-solving and making
  • Digital intelligence and robotics
  • Sustainability and community projects
  • Entrepreneurial awareness and idea showcases
